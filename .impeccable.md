## Design Context

### Users
Decision-makers evaluating high-leverage strategic partners: investors, founders, executive teams, venture partners, and enterprise leaders. They arrive looking for evidence of judgment, range, and operating depth rather than a personal resume. The product should help them quickly understand what this operator builds, how he thinks, and where collaboration could create outsized value.

### Brand Personality
Strategic, minimal, authoritative. The tone should feel composed, exacting, and product-driven rather than self-promotional. Confidence comes from clarity, proof, and restraint.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark mode by default with a cinematic, premium product feel. The interface should sit between Apple, Nike, and a modern venture platform: editorial typography, crisp grids, immersive media, and selective motion. It should avoid generic personal-brand tropes, agency fluff, and resume-style timelines.

### Design Principles
1. Build trust through precision, not volume.
2. Make strategic depth feel tangible with strong structure and real outcomes.
3. Use motion sparingly but memorably, with a few high-impact transitions.
4. Keep the interface product-like: modular, scalable, and intentional.
5. Favor atmosphere and hierarchy over dense information packing.
